Written Scope and Trade Specialists

Each project is carried out by an independent contractor or cabinetmaker registered with the Victorian Building Authority for the class of work involved. The quoting contractor sets out the renovation scope, exclusions and price in writing before any demolition or ordering begins. For owners comparing proposals, that written detail matters more than a polished summary page. Cabinetmaking, stone installation, plumbing and electrical work are handled by specialists in each trade rather than one generalist covering everything. In practice, that means checking who confirms appliance sizes, who manages plumbing changes, who coordinates electrical work around new cabinetry, and what remains excluded until site measure or selection. This separation of trades ensures that the work meets the specific requirements of the building code and the home's structure.
